Step 1: Select Quadrilateral to Correct Perspective

Taken from the side at an angle? Drag the 4 corners (or drag any edge) onto the converging boundaries of your frieze pattern so the grid lines follow the strip. It will be unwarped into a true rectangle.

⚠ Uncross corners

Step 2: Fine-Tune Horizontal Alignment (Optional)

Your quadrilateral has been unwarped into a rectangle! Adjust the tilt angle if needed, or click "2-Point Level" and click two points along a horizontal edge.

Step 3: Identify the Horizontal Middle Line

A transparent flipped upside-down copy appears on top. Drag it up or down on the canvas until its band sits directly on top of the original image to identify the middle line.

Step 4: Determine the Repeat Length (L)

An exact unflipped transparent copy appears on top. Drag it sideways (left or right) by one repeating unit until it matches the original pattern.

Step 3: Record Your Symmetry Findings

Marking a matching symmetry with Space automatically checks Yes below, or you can toggle each transformation manually.

Transformation What to Look For Symmetry?
Horizontal Mirror (H) Flipping upside down across midline matches.
Vertical Mirror (V) Dragging flipped copy left/right clicks onto a vertical mirror line.
180° Rotation (R) Dragging rotated copy left/right clicks onto a rotation centre.
Glide Reflection (G) Flipping across the midline + sliding matches, and neither the flip nor the slide works alone (a true glide, v = L/2). If the midline is a mirror, sliding by kL also works, but that is only mirror + translation.
